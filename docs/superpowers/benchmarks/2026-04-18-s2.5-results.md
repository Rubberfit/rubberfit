## Task 19a — 2D grid warm-start closes the rust-vs-legacy gap (2026-04-18)

**Change:** `warm_start::greedy_bottom_left` now dispatches homogeneous single-Part requests through a deterministic 2D bbox-grid packer. Heterogeneous fixtures still use the Task 11-hardened vertical stacker. Public API unchanged; no GLS/orchestrator changes.

**Result:** all 4 previously-regressing fixtures now match the legacy TS engine placement counts exactly:

| Fixture | Legacy placed | Rust (pre-19a) | Rust (post-19a) | Δ density |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| rect-grid.json           | 20 | 15 | 20 | 0.1172 → 0.1563 (+33%) |
| circle-grid.json         | 12 |  7 | 12 | 0.0849 → 0.1456 (+71%) |
| multi-sheet-remnant.json | 15 | 10 | 15 | 0.1796 → 0.2694 (+50%) |
| overflow.json            |  4 |  2 |  4 | 0.3472 → 0.6944 (+100%) |

The other 5 fixtures (`single-rect`, `multi-rect-same-size`, `trapezoid-mix`, `mixed-rect-circle`, `cone-array`) are unchanged — they were already matching legacy, or they're heterogeneous (multi-part) and route to the stacker.

**Why it works:** all 4 gap fixtures are homogeneous (exactly one `Part` each). The bbox-grid cells of size `(w+2c)×(h+2c)` are non-overlapping under the Task 11 conservative clearance model, so downstream GLS accepts them without modification. Deterministic (pure arithmetic, no RNG).

**Why this design instead of overflow re-seeding:** Codex's sign-off (`.claude/research/archive/task19-fix/codex-report.md`) flagged injection-based approaches as correctness-dangerous. `search::optimize()` discards the feasibility bit from `ExplorationOutcome`; a speculatively-injected overlapping item that GLS fails to separate would come back with `overflow=0` and silently-illegal placements. The 2D grid sidesteps that entirely — it never creates collisions.

Task 18's earlier null-result finding ("GLS + cross-bin orchestrator are NOT the density bottleneck") was correct as far as it went — the bottleneck lived one step earlier in the pipeline, at `warm_start`. Fixing that seam closes the gap without touching the GLS layer.
