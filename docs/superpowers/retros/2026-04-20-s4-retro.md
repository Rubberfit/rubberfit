## What went well

- **Pattern library earned its keep.** By Task 5 we had a stable
  palette of components (`PageShell`, `PageHeader`, `StatCard`,
  `StatusBadge`, `EntityRow`, `ActivityFeedRow`, `DataTable`,
  etc.) that every subsequent surface could consume. By Task 9
  and 10 the surface polish was almost purely composition.
- **Dual sign-off before code.** Gemini + Codex both reviewed the
  S4 plan in `.claude/research/s4-plan/` and blocked on the
  initial design-token scope being too thin. The v2 plan
  (expanded token matrix + full pattern inventory + Vercel Geist
  benchmarks) was the result of their pushback. Codex also
  flagged the `tailwind.config.ts` reference as wrong (the
  project is Tailwind v4; tokens live in `app/globals.css`) —
  that catch saved ~an hour of dead-end work.
- **15 permanent 308 redirects.** Every pre-S4 URL still works
  (procurement, default, materials, cut-planner, nesting-engine,
  account, and the six analytics routes). No broken bookmarks.
- **Pragmatic deferrals.** The planned three-column Cutting
  Engine rebuild (Task 11.5 in v2) moved to S5 to ship with the
  Konva canvas rewrite, because doing the layout shell without
  the canvas redesign would've produced churn. Similarly the
  waste-comparison merge deferred instead of stranding the
  feature without a tab shell.

## What didn't go well

- **Commits stacked up.** 229 files sat uncommitted through
  most of S4 before the user called it. Split retroactively into
  11 task-by-task commits, but cross-cutting files (sidebar,
  permissions, rbac, next.config) had to batch into the Task 10
  commit because hunks were too entangled to split per-task with
  `git add -p`. Next phase: commit after each task, not at the
  end.
- **Naming thrashed mid-phase.** Cut Planner → Cutting Engine →
  Nesting Engine → Cutting Engine. Ended where we started. The
  mock the user shared (image #2) showed "Nesting Engine" which
  triggered the re-think, but the operator feel of "Cutting"
  won in the end. Lost ~30 min of churn. Lesson: commit a name
  on day one; the mock is a reference, not a decree.
- **Nesting vs. Cutting wasn't the only thrash.** The pack-gate
  (Task 5 pre-scope) was investigated, reviewed by Gemini +
  Codex, then discarded when both reviewers concluded the
  quality premise was false on current benchmarks. That's a
  *good* outcome — research saved us from shipping UX theater —
  but the investigation took significant context. Lesson:
  validate premise BEFORE dispatching full dual reviews.
- **Seed-demo iteration.** Took three attempts (anon key, then
  sign-in-with-password, then service role) to get a seed
  script that actually populated the user's account. Should've
  started with the schema inspection — `user_id UUID DEFAULT
  auth.uid()` + RLS was visible in migration 20240223 and
  would've ruled out the first two approaches on day one.

## Notes for the PR review

After S4 the `refactor/rf-engine` branch is PR-ready as an
admin-portal drop. Reviewer focus areas:

1. **308 redirect correctness** in `next.config.mjs` — 15
   entries cover every IA move. Verify no infinite loops.
2. **Permission map** in `src/application/permissions.ts` — new
   nested paths (analytics/*, settings/*, inventory/materials/*)
   all registered. No dangling entries for deleted routes.
3. **RLS implications of seed script** — `seed-demo-data.ts`
   requires `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` to bypass RLS. Must
   stay out of `.env.example` and never ship to the client.
4. **Token layer** — `app/globals.css` is the single source.
   Scan for raw `bg-gray-*` / `text-gray-*` literals; Task 12
   caught the obvious ones but the legacy alias block is a
   visible seam.
5. **Pattern library adoption** — every Task 5–10 page routes
   through `PageShell` + `PageHeader`. Surface regressions are
   most likely to show up where a card or dialog bypassed the
   library.
